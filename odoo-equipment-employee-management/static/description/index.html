🎯 Equipment & Employee Management + AI Module (Odoo 18)

A complete solution to manage IT equipment, employee assignments, loans, and AI-powered suggestions.

🔧 Key Features

✅ Equipment Management

  • Register all types of devices: laptops, phones, tablets, monitors, etc.
  • Detailed information: type, brand, model, serial number, supplier, purchase date, specs, and more.
  • Assign equipment directly to employees with tracking history.
  • Log technical issues or notes per device.

👩‍💼 Employee Management

  • Integrated with Odoo’s HR module (`hr.employee`).
  • See which devices are assigned to each employee.
  • Custom fields for roles, notes, and related hardware.

🔁 Loan Management

  • Track temporary device loans to employees.
  • Start and return dates with status updates.
  • Clear overview of who has which device and for how long.

🤖 AI Assistance (Optional)

  • Basic device health analysis (e.g. based on age or issues).
  • AI recommendations for replacements or updates.
  • Scans comments for repeated technical problems.

⚙️ Technical Details

  • Compatible with Odoo 18.0 Community and Enterprise.
  • Main models: gestio.equipo, gestio.empleat, gestio.prestec.
  • Fully integrated with the hr.employee model.
  • Easy to extend with Inventory, HR, or Helpdesk modules.

📦 Installation

  1. Copy the module into your addons folder.
  2. Restart your Odoo server.
  3. Enable Developer Mode.
  4. Search for gestio_equips_empleats and install it.

🧠 Why Use This Module?

  • Ideal for IT or HR departments that need to manage hardware efficiently.
  • Perfect for companies with equipment rotation or loan processes.
  • Designed for tech companies, schools, government entities, or remote teams.
  • Optional AI features help you decide when to renew or replace devices.
